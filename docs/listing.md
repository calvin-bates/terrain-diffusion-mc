## Description

# Orogeny

**Realistic terrain, generated by AI, built into a whole world.**

Orogeny replaces the overworld with terrain drawn by [Terrain Diffusion](https://github.com/xandergos/terrain-diffusion-mc), a diffusion model trained on real elevation data (SIGGRAPH 2026). Then it builds a world on top: rivers that drain real watersheds, coasts and islands, climates and biomes that follow them, and caves under every mountain. Everything is made from vanilla blocks.

### What you'll find

- **Real-scale terrain.** One block is 5 to 30 real metres; you pick the scale when you create a world. Mountain ranges climb to Y 2031, and the sea floor falls away over a continental shelf into the abyss.
- **Rivers and lakes.** Water drains downhill over the terrain: streams join into navigable rivers that carve their own valleys, basins fill into lakes, and deltas, oxbow lakes, waterfalls and alluvial fans form where the ground calls for them.
- **Oceans and coasts.** Volcanic islands and atolls, reefs and lagoons, barrier islands, sea stacks and arches, sea caves, spits and tombolos, rift seas and deep trenches, iceberg fields and arctic ice islands.
- **85 new biomes, placed by climate.** Boreal forests, rainforests, cloud forests, redwoods, steppe, tundra, salt flats and glaciers, plus showpieces such as volcanoes, canyons, badlands mesas, karst towers, Yellowstone with geysers and Yosemite. They only appear where the terrain already suits them.
- **Caves everywhere.** Caves run under every kind of ground, even inside a peak at Y 1500, with 36 cave biomes: andesite and tuff caves, fungal grottos, deep dark forests, molten caverns, glowworm grottos, giant geode halls, underground lakes, cenotes and natural cave formations. Vanilla's lush caves, deep dark and sulfur caves are in there too.
- **Vanilla structures fit in.** Villages sit on levelled ground, ancient cities in caverns big enough to hold them.
- **Explore before you travel.** `/orogeny-explore` opens a live map of your world in the browser, and `/orogeny-find` takes you to the nearest biome, cave or landmark.

### Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5+, Fabric API, Java 25
- A GPU with about 2 GB of free VRAM (the CPU build works without one, but slowly except on Apple Silicon)
- About 2.5 GB of extra RAM
- An internet connection on first launch to download the terrain models (about 2.5 GB)

### Which file?

| File | Use it on |
|---|---|
| **windows** | Windows with any modern GPU (recommended) |
| **cuda** | NVIDIA GPUs with CUDA and cuDNN installed |
| **cpu** | Everything else; uses CoreML on Apple Silicon Macs |

### Getting started

1. Install Fabric API and the Orogeny jar for your system.
2. Launch once while online so the models download.
3. Create a world: **Orogeny** is already the selected world type. Pick a world scale (2 is a good start).

Tip: with 4 GB or more of free VRAM, set `inference.offload_models=false` in `config/orogeny.properties` for faster generation.
